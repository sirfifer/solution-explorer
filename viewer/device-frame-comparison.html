Notes

  • Level 1: Exactly what ships today. Flat borders, thin hardware hints.
  • Level 2: The "realistic base" you liked. Metallic bezels, status bar, knurled crown, tabs + lock icon. Apple Watch now rectangular with bigger bands.
  • Level 3: Warmer gray screens for notch/bezel contrast. Thicker hardware buttons. Home indicator bar. Larger watch bands. Stronger browser chrome.
  • Level 4: Everything pushed further. Thicker padding/bezels, bolder metallic sheen, more pronounced glow, biggest bands, maximum "instant device recognition."
  • All pure CSS. No SVG, no images. Everything here can ship in React components with Tailwind.
  • Phone is always portrait. Width constrained to 215-230px with min-height 420-470px for a distinctly tall, narrow phone silhouette.
  • Mix and match: You can pick different levels per device type.
